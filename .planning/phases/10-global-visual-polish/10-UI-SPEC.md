# UI-SPEC: Phase 10 - Global Visual Polish

## 1. Visual identity
- Follow `DESIGN.md` as the final authority.
- Keep Lexend as the only font family.
- Eliminate 1px divider lines for sectioning; use tonal surface shifts instead.
- Keep ambient shadows soft, tinted, and sparse.

## 2. Page emphasis rules
- Each page may have at most one true gradient hero or primary stage.
- Glassmorphism belongs to floating navigation and overlay-like layers, not generic content sections.
- Primary CTAs use the brand gradient with rounded-full corners.
- Secondary and tertiary actions stay clearly behind primary CTAs.
- Semantic warning and error panels keep semantic colors first.

## 3. Teacher-side density rules
- Teacher-facing pages should feel like compact command centers.
- Widen the primary information region before cutting content.
- Teacher dashboard must treat daily rhythm and live classroom state as co-primary modules.
- Reuse fewer card styles and keep copy shorter on high-frequency teacher pages.

## 4. Responsive rules
- Desktop is the quality bar for this phase.
- Mobile and tablet must remain coherent, readable, and overflow-free.
- On high-frequency pages, preserve current context, active status, and main actions before secondary metrics.

## 5. Surface families
- Public and student pages may keep brighter showcase moments when they have a natural hero.
- Settings, list-heavy management, and utility pages should prefer calm tonal shells over forced hero treatments.
- Exception styling is limited to hero blocks, live-state modules, and semantic warning or error panels.
